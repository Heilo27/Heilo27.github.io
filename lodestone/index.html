Every Rule. Right There.

The Complete Pathfinder & Starfinder Reference

Lodestone puts the full Pathfinder and Starfinder rulesets at your fingertips — instantly searchable, deeply cross-linked, and fully offline. No more flipping through books mid-combat. No WiFi required.

Built for the Table

The Pathfinder ruleset spans dozens of books and thousands of entries. Finding a feat's prerequisite chain or a monster's full stat block mid-session shouldn't take thirty seconds of frantic page-flipping. Lodestone makes it three.

Instant search. Deep cross-links. Everything offline.

Built to Keep Up With Your Session

Instant Full-Text Search

Search across every spell, feat, class, monster, and item in the ruleset simultaneously. Results appear in under 200ms, grouped by type so you find what you need without hunting. The whole rulebook, searchable in a tap.

Deep Cross-Linking

Every rule reference is a live link. A feat that requires a specific spell links directly to it. A monster's special ability links to the feat it mirrors. Follow the rules wherever they lead — no backtracking, no context lost. The rules as they were meant to be read.

Columnar Hierarchy Navigation

Browse from book to chapter to entry in a fluid columnar layout — especially powerful on iPad. Your navigation context stays visible as you drill down, so you always know where you are and how you got there. Structure that mirrors the rules themselves.

Offline-First

All content lives on your device. No server dependency, no WiFi required, no loading spinners at the table. The Core Rulebook ships with the app — premium content downloads once and stays put. Works wherever you play.

Favorites & Bookmarks

Bookmark any entry and organize it into folders. Build a quick-reference list of your character's spells, your GM prep monsters, or any combination of rules you reach for constantly. Your most-used rules, always one tap away.

iPad & iPhone, Both First-Class

On iPad, the columnar layout keeps your place while you read. On iPhone, a clean navigation stack gives you the same depth in your pocket. Dark mode, Dynamic Type, and VoiceOver supported throughout. Native. Fast. No compromises.

Questions?

What's free vs. premium?

The Core Rulebook — core classes, races, feats, spells, and base rules — is free forever. Premium ($2.99/month) unlocks the full expanded ruleset: all supplements, prestige classes, the complete bestiary, magic items, and advanced systems.

Does the premium subscription cover all three apps?

Yes. One subscription unlocks premium content across Lodestone: PF1, PF2, and SF1 simultaneously.

Does it work offline?

Yes. All content is stored on your device after the initial download. No WiFi, no servers, no loading at the table.

When are PF2 and SF1 coming?

Both are in development. PF2 is next, followed by SF1. The same UI, search, and navigation you know from PF1 carries over to both.

Where does the content come from?

All content is sourced from the Archives of Nethys and d20PFSRD under the Open Game License (OGL). No proprietary Paizo content is included.

Is my data private?

Yes. No analytics, no tracking, no data collection. Your favorites and settings stay on your device.
