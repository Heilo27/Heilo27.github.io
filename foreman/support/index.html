Common Topics

Quick answers to the most frequent questions.

Getting Started

New to Foreman? The app includes a built-in onboarding flow that walks you through setting up your business, adding your first job, and inviting crew members. Open Foreman and follow the setup guide on your first launch.

Billing & Subscriptions

To manage or cancel your subscription, go to Settings → Subscription in the app, or visit iPhone Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions and select Foreman: Field Service.

Data & Privacy

We take your data seriously. Foreman operates offline-first — your job data lives on your device. For full details on what we collect and how we use it, see our Privacy Policy.
