Last updated: November 2026

This policy covers Lodestone PF1, Lodestone PF2 and Lodestone SF1.

The short version: Lodestone collects no data. Everything you do in the app stays on your device.

What Lodestone Stores

Lodestone stores only what you create in the app, on your device:

  • Favorites: entries you save
  • Recently viewed: entries you opened recently
  • Settings: your appearance and app preferences

The rules content is built into the app. Lodestone has no account, no login and no servers. We cannot see or retrieve anything you store.

In-App Purchases

Premium subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple through the App Store. The app asks Apple's StoreKit whether your subscription is active. We never receive your name, Apple ID, email or payment details. Apple handles purchase data under its own privacy policy.

What We Do Not Collect

  • Personal information such as name, email or phone number
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Usage analytics, tracking or behavioral data
  • Location data

Lodestone contains no third-party SDKs, analytics or advertising frameworks. Crash reports reach us only if you choose to share them with developers through Apple.

Deleting Your Data

Deleting the app removes your favorites, history and settings. To cancel a subscription, go to Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions on your device.

Children's Privacy

Lodestone collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we will post the new version here with a new date.
