Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my purchases?

Open the premium screen in the app (the paywall that appears when you open locked content) and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID you subscribed with. If premium still does not unlock, email us.

What is free and what is premium?

The Core Rulebook is free forever. Premium ($1.99 a month or $15.99 a year) unlocks the supplements, the bestiary, magic items and the advanced systems.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are billed and managed by Apple. On your iPhone or iPad, go to Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions, choose Lodestone and manage or cancel it there. We cannot see or change your payment details.

Does one subscription cover PF2 and SF1 too?

No. The PF1 subscription unlocks Lodestone PF1. PF2 and SF1 are still in development.

Does it work offline?

Yes. All content is built into the app, so nothing needs a connection.

Where does the content come from?

Lodestone PF1 is built on rules content published under the Open Game License (OGL). The full license text, copyright notices and content sources are in the app under Legal & Licenses. Lodestone is an independent product and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Paizo Inc.

Is my data private?

Yes. No analytics and no tracking. Favorites and settings stay on your device. Read the privacy policy.
