Foreman is built for the electrician, plumber, or HVAC tech who runs a small crew and needs a real business tool — not enterprise software at enterprise prices.

Everything You Need. Nothing You Don't.

Dispatch Board

See your whole crew's day on one screen. Drag jobs to assign, reschedule with a tap, and push emergency callouts when you need someone on-site fast. iPad-first layout designed for real dispatchers.

Geofence Auto-Timer

Crew members' time logs start the moment they arrive at the job site and stop when they leave. No manual clock-in. No forgotten punch-outs. Your labor costs become accurate automatically.

Route Optimization

Sequence your crew's jobs for the day to minimize drive time. Built with MapKit — no third-party routing fees. Fewer miles, less fuel, more jobs completed before end of day.

Estimates & Invoices

Generate branded estimates and invoices on-site. Email or text them directly to your customer. Collect e-signatures. Track payment status. Send reminders. Your company logo, your numbers, your professional image.

Offline-First

Everything works without a connection. Jobs, time entries, field notes, and photos are stored locally and sync automatically when you're back online. No cell service on the job site? Not a problem.

Onboard crew members with a PIN. Set their role (dispatcher or technician). Track availability. Each crew member sees only their assigned jobs. One app, two experiences — no separate app to maintain.

Questions?

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Foreman is offline-first — every read and write goes to a local database on your device first. Jobs, time entries, photos, and notes are all available without a connection. Changes sync automatically when you reconnect.

Do crew members need a separate subscription?

No. One subscription covers all devices in your company. Crew members download the same app, sign in with a PIN, and see the crew experience automatically based on their role.

How does the geofence timer work?

When you assign a job, Foreman creates a geofence around the job site address. When a crew member's phone enters that area, the time log starts. When they leave, it stops. No action required on their part.

How does QuickBooks sync work?

Connect your QuickBooks Online account in Settings. Invoices sync to QuickBooks when they're marked paid or sent. No double entry. Solo tier users get CSV export for manual import.

What trades is this built for?

Any field service trade: electrical, plumbing, HVAC, general contracting, handyman, painting, flooring, roofing — if you dispatch crew to job sites, Foreman handles it.
