The Problem with Lawn Care Software

Most field management tools cost $150–350/month and lock key features behind their most expensive tiers. Scheduling, invoicing, QuickBooks sync — all separate add-ons. Small operators end up on spreadsheets and group texts, or paying for enterprise complexity they'll never use.

Stripes is built for the operator who runs 1–10 crews and just needs the job done.

Everything You Need

Dispatch in Seconds

Build your crew's daily schedule on iPad. Drag customers onto the day, set job order, and push everything to crew iPhones in one tap. No phone calls. No group texts. Just jobs.

Route Optimization

Stripes calculates the most efficient driving order for each crew's day. Fewer miles. Less fuel. More jobs per day. Automatically sorted, manually adjustable.

Geofence Job Timers

When a crew member arrives at a job site, the timer starts. When they leave, it stops. Accurate time tracking with zero effort. Your labor costs become precise and defensible.

Questions?

Is this a subscription?

Yes. Stripes is $35/month (Calendar) or $79/month (Server). Both tiers offer a 15% discount when billed annually. Cancel any time from the App Store.

What's the difference between tiers?

Calendar Tier uses your iOS calendar as the sync bus — no server, lower cost, works great for 1-2 crews. Server Tier adds cloud sync, real-time updates, photo sharing, and QuickBooks integration for larger operations.

How does QuickBooks work?

Connect your QuickBooks account in Settings. When you mark a job complete, Stripes auto-generates an invoice for your review. Approve it and it's pushed to QuickBooks. No double entry.

Do crew members need to buy anything?

No. One subscription covers all crew devices. Crew members download the free app and connect using an AirDrop authorization (Calendar Tier) or a linking code from the dispatcher (Server Tier).

Does it work without internet?

Calendar Tier uses iOS Calendar sync and works with standard iCloud connectivity. Server Tier requires internet for real-time crew sync, though the crew app caches their job list for offline viewing.

Is it available in Spanish?

Yes. The entire crew app — job details, navigation prompts, timer, notes, and settings — is fully translated into Spanish. The dispatcher app is English.
