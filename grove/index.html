Everything Your Family Needs

Chore Assignments

Create chores for each child with point values, due dates, and recurrence. Kids mark them complete from their own view. Parents approve and award points. No more "did you do your chores?" conversations.

Log positive and negative behavior incidents with context. Grove calculates a weighted behavior score over time, so you can see real trends — not just today's mood. Patterns you can actually act on.

Kids earn points for chores, good behavior, and bonus moments. Points drive a multiplier that affects their reward rate — giving kids a real reason to stay consistent. Motivation that builds on itself.

Questions?

Is Grove free?

Grove is free to download and try. A subscription unlocks the full feature set for your whole family — chore tracking, behavior scoring, goals, and multi-child support.

How many kids can I add?

As many as you need. Each child gets their own profile, chore list, behavior score, and point balance.

Can kids use it independently?

Yes — that's the point. Kids see their own chores and can mark them complete. Parents review and approve from their own view.

What is the behavior score?

Grove tracks behavior incidents over a rolling window and calculates a weighted score. Recent behavior counts more than older incidents, so improvement shows up quickly.

Can I set up recurring chores?

Yes. Set chores to repeat daily, weekly, or on specific days of the week. They auto-refresh so you don't have to recreate them.

Where is my data stored?

On your device and synced through your own iCloud. We don't have access to your family's data.
