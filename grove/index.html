Structure Is How Kids Learn to Flourish

Child psychologists have long understood that kids don't just benefit from structure — they need it. Consistent routines, immediate positive feedback, and visible progress toward meaningful goals are the building blocks of self-confidence, responsibility, and intrinsic motivation.

Grove puts those principles into practice, every single day.

Every Feature Grounded in How Kids Grow

Consistent Daily Routines

Assign chores with point values, due dates, and automatic recurrence. Kids see exactly what's expected of them every day — building the habit of follow-through that carries far beyond the household. Consistency today becomes character tomorrow.

Behavior That Builds Over Time

Grove tracks behavior using a weighted score that emphasizes recent growth over past missteps. Progress is always visible and always within reach — giving kids a genuine sense that improvement is worth the effort. Growth is the goal, not perfection.

Positive Reinforcement That Works

Kids earn points for completing chores, showing great behavior, and going above and beyond. A behavior multiplier means sustained effort is genuinely rewarded — not just acknowledged. Good choices feel worth making.

Goals That Teach Delayed Gratification

Set meaningful goals for each child — a coveted toy, a special outing, extra screen time. Watching their progress bar fill is a daily reminder that effort leads to reward. One of the most valuable lessons a kid can learn.

Every Child, Individually Supported

Each child gets their own profile, routines, behavior score, and goals — tailored to where they are developmentally. Celebrate each child's growth on their own terms, not in comparison to their siblings. Every kid's journey is their own.

Parents Set the Standard

You define the expectations. Kids rise to meet them. Approve completions, award bonuses for exceptional effort, and keep the full picture of each child's progress — all from your own parent view. Guided autonomy. Kids own their effort, parents own the outcomes.

Rooted in How Kids Actually Develop

Immediate, Meaningful Feedback

Research consistently shows that children respond best to immediate, specific positive reinforcement. Grove awards points the moment a chore is approved — closing the loop between effort and reward.

Routine as a Foundation

Predictable daily routines reduce anxiety and improve focus in children. When kids know what's expected and what comes next, they spend less energy resisting and more energy doing.

Private and Safe for Families

Your family's data stays on your devices. No advertising, no data selling, no third-party access. Grove is a tool for your family — not a platform built around them.

Questions?

How does pricing work?

Grove requires a subscription to use. Download the app and subscribe to unlock chore tracking, behavior scoring, goals, and multi-child support for your whole family.

How many kids can I add?

As many as you need. Each child gets their own profile, chore list, behavior score, and point balance.

Can kids use it independently?

Yes — that's the point. Kids see their own chores and can mark them complete. Parents review and approve from their own view.

How does the behavior score work?

Grove tracks behavior over a rolling window and weights recent actions more heavily than older ones. That means improvement shows up quickly — kids aren't defined by their worst week, and consistent growth is always rewarded.

Can I set up recurring chores?

Yes. Set chores to repeat daily, weekly, or on specific days of the week. They auto-refresh so you don't have to recreate them.

Where is my data stored?

On your device and synced through your own iCloud. We don't have access to your family's data.
